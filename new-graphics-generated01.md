## 📐 Global Art Direction & Styling Parameters

When generating or replacing these assets, maintain consistent styling:
* **Style**: Gritty 90s NYC Hip-Hop & Cyber-Graffiti comic book art style.
* **Linework**: Bold, crisp black comic ink outlines, cel-shaded lighting, clean alpha transparency.
* **Palette**: Raw urban concrete/asphalt tones accented with electric aerosol neon colors:
  * **Mint Turquoise / Cyan**: `#4CF5D5`
  * **Hot Magenta**: `#FF3B77`
  * **Chrome Gold**: `#FFE06D`
  * **Acid Hazard Lime**: `#76FF03`
  * **Electric Purple**: `#B455FF`
  * **Hazard Warning Orange/Red**: `#FF4158`
* **Perspective**: Top-Down 2D (slight ~75-80° bird's-eye angle so vehicles, characters, and props have readable silhouettes and depth).
* **Format**: Isolated sprite with clean **transparent background (PNG)**, centered framing, no borders or drop-shadow clipping.
