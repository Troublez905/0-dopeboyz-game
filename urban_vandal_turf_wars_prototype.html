Street Shooters

Graffiti Turf Prototype

Run the city, tag walls, hold territory, grab paint, recruit backup, fight rivals, and keep away from cops.

Finish each level task to earn upgrade credits. Spend them on character skills, crew strength, headquarters perks, and unlocked features.

Busted means jail time and lost items. Dropped means hospital time. Rivals keep spraying while you are gone.

Move with WASD or arrows. Hold Space to paint. Shift sprints. Press F to shoot if you have ammo. Walk into cans, med packs, weapons, and recruits.

City Claimed

Your crew owns the night.

Replay the block and try to hold every wall longer.

Field Guide

Beta Rules

Claim walls, hold territory, collect supplies, recruit backup, and finish each level objective before the city takes the block back.

WASD / arrows move through streets and alleys.
Hold Space near a wall to paint. Bigger walls take longer.
F fires your weapon. Ammo is limited, so make shots count.
Shift sprints. Paint cans, health, ammo, weapons, and recruits glow on the map.
Police bust you and seize items. Rivals hurt you and spray over your work.
Level credits upgrade character skills, gang backup, HQ perks, and weapons.
