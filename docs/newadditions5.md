# Street Shooters: 25 New Gameplay & Visual Additions (Batch 5)

This document outlines **25 comprehensive game-driven and visual mechanics** designed to expand **Street Shooters** with advanced aerial mobility, tactical SWAT encounters, DJ scratch battles, aerosol flamethrowers, custom stencil tuning, and authentic urban graffiti culture.

---

## 1. Graffiti Paint Glider Wingsuit (`Hold Space in Air`)

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Jump off building rooftops and deploy a custom aerosol wingsuit frame to glide across city blocks while spraying continuous paint trails below.
* **Prompt**: High-tech canvas wingsuit frame with dual pressurized aerosol thruster nozzles, neon cyan wing ribs, and wind trail particle ribbons.

---

## 2. Police SWAT Armored Tactical Breaching Van (`Heat Level 5 Event`)

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: At max heat (Level 5 pursuit), a heavy SWAT armored truck arrives on major avenues, deploying riot shield officers and launching tear gas canisters that obscure vision and slow movement.
* **Prompt**: Matte black armored SWAT riot van with heavy steel plating, bulletproof windshield mesh, flashing red/blue strobe lights, and tear gas launcher.

---

## 3. Subway Train Top Surfing & Tunnel Bombing

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Hop onto moving subway train cars passing along elevated tracks to tag moving train panels for massive crew score multipliers.
* **Prompt**: High-speed silver subway train car with glowing headlight beam, electrical sparks from third rail, and side graffiti panel canvas.

## 4. Adrenaline Overdrive Slow-Motion Paint Precision (`Key Shift + F`)

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Trigger an adrenaline surge that slows down time by 50% for 6 seconds, enabling precision aiming and instant stencil tagging during intense firefights.
