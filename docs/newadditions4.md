# Street Shooters: 20 New Gameplay & Visual Additions (Batch 4)

This document outlines **20 comprehensive game-driven and visual mechanics** designed to elevate **Street Shooters** with deep tactical urban combat, advanced mobility, dynamic weather hazards, custom stencil workshops, and authentic graffiti street culture.

---

## 1. Deployable Paint Cannon Turret (`Key N`)

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Player can deploy an automated, stationary paint turret on pavement or rooftops. It automatically targets incoming police and rival squads with high-pressure aerosol splatters while auto-tagging adjacent walls within its radius.
* **Prompt**: Automated heavy tripod turret with dual pressurized paint tanks, glowing cyan nozzle tip, ammo belt feeder, and rotating target sensor array.

---

## 2. Underground Sewer & Subway Secret Passages (`Shift + E at Manhole`)

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Manhole covers and sewer grates in back alleys can be opened to instantly drop underground, evading 5-star police pursuits and bypassing heavy street blockades.
* **Prompt**: Weathered cast-iron manhole cover with glowing green sewer steam rising through grates, dripping moss, and street hazard warning paint.

---

## 3. Rooftop Cable Zipline & Grind Rails (`Space on Cable`)

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Player can hook onto rooftop fire escape cables and overhead street wires to grind across building gaps at extreme speed, allowing rapid vertical aerial mobility.
* **Prompt**: Overhead steel cable line with neon sparks flying from roller-skate grind clamps, glowing zipline trolley pulley handle, and motion blur trail.

---

## 4. Chopper Police Searchlight Radar Evading Thermal Foil (`Key B`)

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Deploy a metallic thermal foil blanket when police helicopters hover overhead to hide heat signatures from high-altitude spotters and break tracking locks.
* **Prompt**: Metallic chrome reflective thermal blanket decal, shimmering infrared distortion aura, and stealth sensor disruption particles.

---

## 5. Tactical Paint Splatter Mine Traps (`Key Alt + X`)

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Plant pressure-sensitive paint landmines along alley floors. When rivals or police step on them, they detonate in a massive 360° sticky paint splash that slows enemy movement by 60% and inflicts damage.
* **Prompt**: Pressure-plate aerosol can landmine prop with glowing red sensor laser, hazard wire, and splash radius indicator ring.

---

## 6. Custom Stencil Workshop & Crew Logo Editor

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: In-game stencil studio allowing players to design custom crew tags that provide unique passive bonuses (e.g., +20% cash from claimed walls, +15% crew health, -10% heat accumulation).
* **Prompt**: Retro 16-bit stencil canvas UI layout with spray paint grid, layer controls, color palette swatches, and test wall preview.

---

## 7. Street News Broadcast Helicopter & Live Crime Feed

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: A news TV helicopter flies overhead during high-heat engagements, broadcasting a live news ticker HUD that boosts crew popularity and score multipliers when pulling off stylish maneuvers.
* **Prompt**: News network TV spotlight helicopter prop with twin rotor blades, camera lens pod, floating news headline ticker banner, and TV camera frame HUD.

---

## 8. Rival Boss Captain Spray Showdown Encounters

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Capturing 80% of a district summons a Rival Boss Captain who actively attempts to repaint your highest value walls in real-time duel encounters.
* **Prompt**: Aggressive rival gang leader sprite wearing customized spiked leather jacket, respirator mask, dual gold spray cans, and skull gang crest overlay.

---

## 9. High-Pressure Air Compressor Refill Stations

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Industrial air compressor stations located at alley hideouts that instantly recharge paint tanks to 100% capacity and grant a temporary paint velocity pressure boost.
* **Prompt**: Industrial red air compressor tank station with pressure gauge dial, coiled rubber hose, brass nozzle coupler, and blue steam pressure vents.

---

## 10. Customizable Spray Can Nozzles (6 Nozzle Cap Types)

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Equip specialized nozzles (Monster Fat Cap, Calligraphy Chisel, Needlecaps, Soft Caps) with distinct spray cone angles, paint speeds, and stamina costs.
* **Prompt**: 6 distinct spray nozzle cap models: Ultra-Wide Monster Cap, Precision Needle Cap, Chisel Calligraphy Tip, Soft Fanning Cap, Splatter Nozzle, and Flare Nozzle.

---

## 11. Alley Trash Can Fire Warmup Zones

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: Standing near glowing alley barrel fires regenerates health and stamina faster during cold night cycles and resets freeze slowdown debuffs.
* **Prompt**: Corrugated steel trash barrel fire prop with rising orange flame sparks, glowing embers, smoke plumes, and warm radial lighting halo.

## 12. Police K9 Tracker Units & Scent Disruption Aerosol

* **Gameplay Mechanic**: High heat levels spawn police German Shepherd K9 units that follow your scent trail unless disrupted by throwing pepper paint bombs.
* **Prompt**: Armored police K-9 German Shepherd sprite with tactical vest, glowing red collar LED, motion trail, and scent trail visual lines.
