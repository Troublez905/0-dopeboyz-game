# Street Shooters: Graphics Asset Pipeline & Prompt Catalog

This document specifies all new graphical assets required for **Street Shooters (Turf Wars Expansion)**. Each entry includes detailed visual specs, target dimensions, palette/style guides, and ready-to-use generative AI image prompts.

---

## Art Direction & Style Guidelines

- **Core Aesthetic**: 90s NYC / Cyberpunk Hip-Hop Street Culture & Raw Graffiti Realism.
- **Visual Feel**: Bold comic inked outlines, vibrant neon spray paint accents (Mint Cyan `#4cf5d5`, Hot Magenta `#ff3b77`, Chrome Gold `#ffe06d`, Acid Lime `#76ff03`, Electric Purple `#b455ff`), contrasted against dark textured asphalt and weathered brick walls.
- **Camera Perspective**: Top-Down 2D (slight angled perspective for character heads/shoulders, top-down for vehicles and road decals).
